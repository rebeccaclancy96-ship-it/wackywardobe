# Wacky Wardrobe

A kid-friendly dress-up game (ages 3+) starring the dino-onesie character from *Wacky Playoffs – Kids Edition*. Native SwiftUI iOS app; everything is saved on the device.

## Features
- **Pick your character**: Boy, Girl or "Just Me" (neutral), plus an optional name.
- **Pet buddy**: choose a first pet for free (Puppy, Kitty, Bunny, Piggy) and name it. More pets cost coins.
- **Special-edition pets**: Rainbow Unicorn (120 coins) and Jewel Turtle (100 coins), featured first in the Pets collection with SPECIAL badges. Permanent coin unlocks, not timed exclusives or direct real-money purchases. Both have original transparent artwork for all seven pet looks (plain, pajamas, rainbow, strawberry, bee, dino, hero), use existing saved ownership/outfits, and accompany the player in Buddy Time and learning games. Onboarding retains the four free starter choices. Pet Match now uses six pet pairs, preserving its 12-card size.

- **Costumes** (8): Dino (free), Shark, Banana, Unicorn, Taco, Chicken, Pickle, Astronaut, drawn for each of the 3 characters.
- **Pet outfits** (4 + none): Berry Hat, Bumblebee, Mini Dino, Super Cape, drawn for every pet.
- **Places** (5): Rainbow (free), Under the Sea, Dino Jungle, Outer Space, Candy Land, drawn in code.
- **Coins**: start with 50. Buy items in the wardrobe; if you're short, the app sends you to the games.
- **Mini-games**: Star Catch (30s tapping game), Gem Garden (match-three swaps and cascades; up to 60 coins), Pet Match (memory pairs; fewer moves = more coins), Lucky Spin (free prize wheel every 3 hours).

- Tap the character to make them wiggle, or the pet to make it hop. Confetti when you buy something.
- **Everyday outfits**: free Everyday Tee, Sunny Overalls (35), Starry PJs (45), Rainbow Day (55), for all three kid types.
- **Match Us!**: Sleepover Stars, Rainbow Buddies and Dino Duo dress the kid and selected pet together; only unowned pieces cost coins. Pajamas (25) and rainbow (30) pet outfits are available for all four species.
- **Buddy Time**: an animated touch-driven playground showing the exact saved kid, costume (including shiny finishes), pet, pet outfit and scene together. Play mode supports tap-to-walk, dragging, gentle wandering, bobbing/hopping and a dance button; care mode steadies the selected target. Food/water follow the finger, brush/wash strokes require contact with the buddy, and hearts/sparkles/bubbles respond. Food choice changes the displayed snack. Saved clothes stay on for every tool (brush hair or costume hood). Three care interactions complete a persisted happy moment, with no timer or penalties. Reduce Motion suppresses idle motion, walking bounce and dance bounce. Uses animated 2D character sprites, not skeletal/3D limb animation.

## Daily gifts and shiny editions
- Home's Daily gifts button opens a seven-visit card. Claim once per UTC calendar day: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, then 60 coins and a shiny edition. Missed days do not reset progress; after visit 7, a new card starts on the next eligible day.
- Three monthly rotating editions: Golden Dino, Prism Unicorn, Starlight Explorer. These reuse existing character art with a native pearlescent finish and animated glints (Reduce Motion respected). Editions may return; a started card locks its edition through completion. Owned duplicates grant 50 extra coins. Unlocked editions remain wearable in the wardrobe and cannot be purchased.
- Claims atomically persist the day, progress, coins and outfit ownership in existing SaveData via an optional backward-compatible field. No account or backend required. This is a device-local reward calendar using the device clock, not server-authoritative anti-cheat or cross-device login tracking.
- Verified current source on iPhone simulator: shiny preview, visit 1 claim (+10, 1,753 → 1,763), disabled repeat claim, and progress/balance retained after relaunch. Debug rule checks passed for same-day duplicates, clock rollback, missed days, month changes, seventh-visit edition and next-card rollover. Actual seven-day waiting and equipping a newly unlocked edition through UI remain unverified.

## Structure (ios/vibecodedapp)
- `Models/`: Catalog (costumes, pets, outfits), SceneStyle, SaveData, WardrobeItem
- `ViewModels/`: GameStore (coins, owned items, saved to UserDefaults), Home, StarCatch, GemGarden, MatchGame, LuckySpin
- `Views/`: Onboarding, Home (stage, wardrobe panel, buy sheet, settings), Games (hub + 4 games + reward card)
- `Components/`: SceneBackground, StageView, CoinBadge, ConfettiView
- `Theme.swift`: palette, rounded fonts, chunky toy-style buttons, haptics
- Art: `Assets.xcassets/kid_<boy|girl|kid>_<costume>` and `pet_<pet>_<outfit>`, generated with GPT Image from the original character (script: `backend/tools/generate-wardrobe.ts`).

## Optional coin purchases
- Custom SwiftUI coin shop, always behind a fresh randomized grown-up addition challenge.
- Consumables: 100 coins / US$0.99, 500 / US$2.99, 1,200 / US$4.99; prices displayed from Apple products in the local currency. No subscriptions.
- RevenueCat `coin_shop` offering; Apple product prefix `com.vibecodeapp.app_4thf0g3bzcmz.coins.`. No premium entitlement (coins are consumables).
- `CoinShopModel` configures the App Store SDK, loads packages, handles cancellation/pending/errors and reconciles purchase history. `GameStore.creditPurchase` persists transaction IDs and balance atomically in the existing save, preventing duplicate credits on relaunch. Existing saves migrate without resetting progress.
- Reachable from the Home coin badge, Settings, and an insufficient-coins item sheet. Free games remain available.
- Balance/outfits live on this device; no cross-device cloud wallet or spent-consumable restore is promised. Deleting app data may lose progress.
- Simulator purchase evidence: all three US/USD packs completed in Environment: Xcode and appeared in the exact RevenueCat anonymous customer's sandbox purchases. Balance 26 → 126 → 626 → 1,826; persisted without duplicate credit after relaunch; Banana outfit bought for 50 → 1,776.
