Wacky Wardrobe Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Wacky Wardrobe (the app) is provided by Rebecca Clancy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. Questions go to rebeccaclancy96@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide in the app, such as text, audio, photos, or other content you create or enter.
  • Basic technical information, such as device type and iOS version, used to keep the app working.
  • If you make a purchase, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use information

To provide, maintain, and improve the app, and to respond to your requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help run the app, such as hosting, payments, or analytics, and only as needed for that purpose. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app. Deleting the app removes data stored on your device. To have your information deleted, email rebeccaclancy96@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
